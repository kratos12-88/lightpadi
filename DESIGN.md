# LightPadi visual identity

Energy utility — electric yellow, near-black and warm white; bento service tiles, pill navigation and oversized service icons.

Palette: accent: `#806000`, tint: `#fff1a9`, ink: `#242414`, paper: `#f4f4ed`

The product has its own navigation treatment, content composition, typography hierarchy, and component styling. Shared functional behaviour remains local to this independent repository. No gradients. Responsive rules adapt the layout at 1100, 900, 700 and/or 400 pixels as appropriate. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
